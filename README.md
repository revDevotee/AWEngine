
# AfterWorld Engine(AWEngine)[![dotnet](https://img.shields.io/badge/.net-10.0-purple)](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0)

[English](#english) | [Русский](#russian)

<a id="english"></a>

Something along the lines of a C# engine/framework for TERMINAL games, or honestly, whatever you want if you figure it out. The main idea is to quickly and easily slap together a simple console game, and then, if you feel like it, you could theoretically add graphics.

Works kind of like an ECS (Entity Component System).

## Additional information

If you’re wondering, 'Why does this even exist when there are plenty of other great engines out there?' Simply put: because I can. It's for people who need the absolute most obscure engine just to be different, or for anyone who’s too lazy to build everything from scratch and just wants a super simple engine with a super simple implementation—or honestly, for anyone at all. It’s not aiming to be any kind of killer, not even among obscure engines. It just exists.

# AfterWorld Engine(AWEngine)[![dotnet](https://img.shields.io/badge/.net-10.0-purple)](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0)

Что то в духе C# движка/фреймворка для разработки ТЕРМИНАЛЬНЫХ игр, или чего угодно если разобраться. Основная идея максимально просто и быстро создать не сложную консольную игру, а дальше если захотеть то это теоретически можно сделать с графикой.

Работает на подобии ECS(Entity Component System)
